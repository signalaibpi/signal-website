Executive, precise, restrained — a light canvas with a blue accent used sparingly for primary actions and diagnostic priorities, generous whitespace, and a strict grid with softened corners. Built with Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS (see `TECHNICAL.md`).

The homepage and Business AI Diagnostic page use the same moderately compact spacing between sections; spacing within each section remains more generous for readability.

Visual design source of truth: `/Users/erikdekroon/Documents/Signal/Website/Signal Website Design Guidelines.md` (and `Gemini Image Style Guidelines.md` for imagery). Follow the design-doc split above — don't restate raw styling constants (colors, spacing, etc.) from those guidelines here; only document a visual fact once it carries behavioral meaning for a specific page.

When UI behavior, placement, visible labels, or page structure changes, update this document in the same change set.

## Authentication
There is no login. The site is fully public.

## Pages

## Homepage

Route: `/`

### Purpose
- Explain what Signal does and why it's different (frontline ownership of AI results).
- Introduce the Business AI Diagnostic and Signal's other two services (AI Strategy Planning, Embedded AI Delivery).
- Build credibility (founder background) and give visitors a clear next step.

### Sections (in order)
1. **Hero** — headline ("Take control of your AI progress"), followed by two short paragraphs: "Signal helps CEOs and COOs improve frontline AI adoption." and "Signal’s diagnostic identifies what’s holding adoption back. Our planning and delivery services turn those insights into improved business performance." The hero retains the primary CTA ("Book a free AI Snapshot"), secondary CTA ("View a sample diagnostic"), and illustrative Business AI Diagnostic sample card.
2. **Our approach** — explains why frontline teams need to take ownership of AI results, with a real photo (`/home-our-approach.png`, 4:3) alongside the copy.
3. **Business AI Diagnostic** — three cards describing what the Diagnostic does across all 12 dimensions (finds issues, prioritises, sets targets). Defines what the Diagnostic is, ahead of the "when it applies" section below — Erik moved this up from originally sitting after it, since explaining usage before the reader knows what the thing is read backwards. The cards originally carried "Diagnostic dimensions 01–04" / "05–08" / "09–12" tags inherited from the original design sample — removed as factually wrong: each card describes something that happens across all 12 dimensions at once, not a step tied to a subset of them.
4. **"When the Diagnostic applies"** — three cards showing the Diagnostic applying at three different stages of AI adoption (planning a new use case, diagnosing an underperforming pilot, scaling something that's working). Not generic product benefits — renamed from "Benefits" since that label and its original header ("Getting more value from AI requires different answers at different stages") described neither accurately. Note: its three cards cover similar ground to the Business AI Diagnostic section's own three cards (existing-vs-new applications, target-setting) — flagged as a possible content overlap worth revisiting, not yet resolved. Has a full-width Collaborate-style photograph between the intro text and the card grid (mirroring How it works' layout), showing a working discussion led by an experienced colleague in an ordinary ground-floor office. It breaks up three consecutive card-grid sections; the adjacent Business AI Diagnostic and Other Services sections remain image-less.
5. **Other services** — AI Strategy Planning and Embedded AI Delivery modules. Neither module has its own CTA; only the section-level "Explore Signal services" link at the bottom is clickable — a deliberate single-CTA-per-section simplification, not an oversight.
6. **Who is this for** — audience paragraph, then sector tags (Fintech, Software, Outsourcing, Professional Services), then a 16:9 Atmosphere-style photograph of professionals working across a busy open-plan office. The environment and layered activity are the focus rather than any individual person. This sector list no longer matches `Signal Website Copy.md`'s literal sentence ("software, professional services, distribution, and outsourcing") — Erik replaced Distribution with Fintech, which is the Business Plan's #1 priority sector and was oddly missing from that sentence. `Signal Website Copy.md` hasn't been updated to match; treat the live site as current until that's reconciled. The right column has extra top padding (`lg:pt-10`) so its content starts level with the left column's H2 rather than its small eyebrow label above the H2 — Erik flagged the unpadded version as visually awkward (text starting level with a tiny eyebrow next to it, not the headline).
7. **Founder** — a headshot photo with a name/title byline in the left column, and the eyebrow, headline and bio copy stacked together in the right column (headline directly above the body text, not in a separate column from it). The photo has a plain border, no accent-color frame — an earlier `bg-primary` frame was tried and dropped as visually heavy.
8. **How it works** — a full-width 21:9 Collaborate-style photograph of three colleagues prioritising workstreams on a planning wall, followed by four numbered engagement steps, each labelled in small caps top-right with the Signal product it corresponds to (AI Snapshot, Business AI Diagnostic, AI Strategy Planning, Embedded AI Delivery), so the steps read as an explicit map onto the four packages rather than a generic process. The step-01 badge no longer carries a distinct blue highlight — all four number badges use the same neutral style, since there's no actual "current step" state on a static page and singling one out implied an asymmetry that doesn't exist between the steps.
9. **Final CTA** — repeats both hero CTAs.

There is no separate "Problem" section — `Signal Website Design Guidelines.md`'s homepage section sequence calls for one between Hero and Our Approach, but `Signal Website Copy.md` has no copy for it. Erik confirmed (2026-09-18) that Benefits, one section later, covers that narrative role instead; the Design Guidelines doc hasn't been updated to match.

The header's Signal logo links to `/` from every page and replaces the header's "Homepage" menu item. On narrower screens, a menu button replaces the desktop navigation and header CTA; it opens the same navigation links in a vertical panel, closes when a link is chosen or Escape is pressed, and does not repeat the CTA (which appears in page content). The footer retains its "Homepage" link. Other links — nav, footer, and inline links like Our Approach's "Read more about how to build frontline ownership of AI" or the Diagnostic/Other Services section links — point to `#`, except the nav and footer's "Business AI Diagnostic" link, which points to `/business-ai-diagnostic`. The rest will be wired up as they're built.

The browser favicon is a compact Signal monogram: a dark "S" followed by the site's blue dot. It preserves the dot from the wordmark while remaining identifiable when the site is one of several open tabs.

### Visual facts that carry meaning
- The hero's Business AI Diagnostic card shows all 12 real dimensions as a grid of numbered tiles (number + name, no scores). Each tile is tinted by which of the diagnostic's 4 areas the dimension belongs to (Strategy/Implementation/Operation/Governance — see `Signal Business AI Diagnostic.md` → Areas), with the area name in small caps top-right of the tile. This intentionally departs from Design Guidelines' "use neutral tones, accent reserved for a selected dimension/priority" rule — Erik decided area color-coding was worth it over an all-grey grid; a first neutral version is preserved in git history (`components/home/Hero.tsx` as of commit `119b028`) if the guideline's stricter reading is ever preferred instead. Dimension names and area assignments must always match the real 12 dimensions (see `Signal Business Plan.md` → Business AI Diagnostic).
- The 4 area colors are pale washes, kept deliberately subtle (final, after Erik felt an earlier louder pass looked "too dark"): Strategy `#2457d6` on `#dfe8f8` (the site's primary blue), Implementation `#145a7d` on `#e1eff6` (steel blue — nudged from an earlier, more cyan shade that read as green-ish), Operation `#3e4759` on `#eceef2` (slate gray), Governance `#6d51c9` on `#eae4f5` (lavender). Defined as `--color-area-*` / `--color-area-*-tint` tokens in `app/globals.css`. Green, amber and yellow are deliberately excluded from all 4 — those are reserved by the diagnostic's own RAG status system (`Signal Business AI Diagnostic.md` → Output), and reusing them here would collide with that meaning. These are the same colors planned for the future Diagnostic page's radial chart — see `Website/Business AI Diagnostic page/Visual ideas.md`.
- The hero has no image — the 12-dimension card is the only hero visual, top-aligned with the headline column. It originally sat layered over a placeholder photo, but once the card grew to show all 12 dimensions it was taller than that pairing could support without leaving a dead gap below the CTAs; removing the (still-unsourced) placeholder photo fixed the imbalance directly rather than just repositioning the card. Photography still appears elsewhere on the page (Our Approach, Who is this for, How it works).
- The diagnostic card has no internal "View a sample diagnostic" link — an earlier version had one, but it duplicated the hero's own secondary CTA button (identical text, same placeholder destination) a few inches away. Removed as pure redundancy. The pill ("Powered by the Business AI Diagnostic") and the secondary CTA are exact copy from `Signal Website Copy.md` and stay; the card's own "Business AI Diagnostic" title label stays too since it's the only thing identifying what the tile grid represents.

## Business AI Diagnostic

Route: `/business-ai-diagnostic`

### Purpose
- Explain what the Business AI Diagnostic does and make its assessment tangible before someone books time with Signal.
- Show all 12 dimensions the Diagnostic scores against, with enough detail (weight, what it measures, the underlying questions) to make the framework credible without overwhelming a first-time visitor.

### Sections (in order)
1. **Hero** — badge, headline ("Make your AI transformation measurable"), lead paragraph, single CTA ("View a sample diagnostic" — the copy doc defines only one CTA for this page's hero, unlike the homepage's two). Visual is a 12-wedge radial diagram (`DimensionsRadial`), one wedge per dimension, colour-coded by the same 4 area tokens as the homepage's dimension grid. Wedge length is illustrative only (not a real score or the dimension's weight) — see the code comment in `DimensionsRadial.tsx`.
2. **Why it applies** — the 3 panels from `Signal Website Copy.md` (Prioritise with a realistic view of readiness / Find what is holding AI back / Give teams clear improvement targets), styled like the homepage's benefit-card pattern but with this page's own copy (not a shared component).
3. **The 12 diagnostic dimensions** — intro paragraphs, then a grid of 12 interactive cards, one per dimension, in the same numbered order as the homepage's hero tile grid.

### Visual facts that carry meaning
- Each dimension card is a compact tile — a small number and area label (same font size as each other) above a bold name set in `on-surface-variant` gray (not near-black `on-surface`, and not area-coloured) — full black at this size/weight read as too heavy, so it uses the same softer gray as the site's body copy. The name is large enough that longer dimension names wrap to two lines on purpose (e.g. "Workforce AI Capability", "Policy and Control Assurance") rather than being shrunk to fit one line; the tile has a `min-h` tall enough for two lines so every card in a grid row still matches height whether its own title wraps or not. The number stays area-coloured for identity, but the title itself is deliberately kept neutral rather than area-coloured too — colouring both would make some cards look more prominent than others based on hue alone (e.g. Operation's muted slate vs. Strategy's vivid blue) rather than meaning. A small "View details →" label anchored at the bottom-right makes the interaction explicit while remaining subordinate to the dimension name; on pointer hover and keyboard focus, the card lifts slightly and gains a clearer border and shadow. Clicking/tapping a card opens a centered pop-up dialog (generous `p-12` padding so the text doesn't feel cramped; dimmed backdrop, closes on the X button, Escape, or a backdrop click) in the same area tint as the tile: number and "AREA, Weight N" on one line, the name repeated, a bold summary sentence, an "Example questions include:" label (the one piece of UI copy on this page that isn't from `Signal Website Copy.md` — added directly by Erik to make clear what the bullets below it are), then the dimension's underlying questions — everything else verbatim from the copy doc. This replaced two earlier approaches tried in this build: a drop-down overlay panel, then an in-place 3D flip — both were dropped because forcing every card to share one box size (needed for the flip) left most cards mostly empty to accommodate the longest dimension's content. The pop-up sizes itself to its own content instead, so short and long dimensions both fit naturally. No hover reveal — a pop-up appearing on mouseover would be too aggressive, so this is click/tap-only, which also sidesteps the hover-vs-touch race conditions the earlier approaches had to work around.
- Weight shown in the pop-up is the plain number from `Signal Website Copy.md` only (e.g. "Weight 4") — never a named tier (Supporting/Contributing/Major/Determining). Those names exist in `Signal Business AI Diagnostic.md` but that's the internal methodology doc, not approved website copy, so they're deliberately excluded from this page.
- All hero, panel, and dimension text is sourced verbatim from `Signal Website Copy.md`'s "Business AI Diagnostic" section, with one exception noted above (the pop-up's "Example questions include:" label) — nothing on this page is invented or pulled from the internal methodology doc.
- Photography is not sourced yet; this page currently has no image placeholders because none of its three sections call for one in the copy doc (unlike the homepage).

### States
- No loading, error, or empty states — the page is fully static with no data fetching.

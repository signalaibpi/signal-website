Executive, precise, restrained — a light canvas with a blue accent used sparingly for primary actions and diagnostic priorities, generous whitespace, and a strict grid with softened corners. Built with Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS (see `TECHNICAL.md`).

The homepage, Services, Business AI Dashboard and Who we help pages use the same moderately compact spacing between sections; spacing within each section remains more generous for readability.

Visual design source of truth: `/Users/erikdekroon/Documents/Signal/Website/Signal Website Design Guidelines.md` (and `Gemini Image Style Guidelines.md` for imagery). Follow the design-doc split above — don't restate raw styling constants (colors, spacing, etc.) from those guidelines here; only document a visual fact once it carries behavioral meaning for a specific page.

When UI behavior, placement, visible labels, or page structure changes, update this document in the same change set.

There is no login. The site is fully public.

## Services

Route: `/services`

### Purpose
- Explain Signal's three services — AI Progress Review, AI Planning and AI Delivery — what each involves and what it delivers.
- Show that the services build on each other and that a client can stop after any phase.

### Sections (in order)
1. **Hero** — H1 “How we make AI work for your teams”, the introduction, the three services as a list of rows (bold name, colon, one-line summary, arrow) that each jump to that service's section, and the sentence about the services building on each other. An image placeholder sits in the right column on desktop and below the copy on mobile. No CTA button.
2. **AI Progress Review** (`#ai-progress-review`, “Step 01”) — two equal, top-aligned columns: on the left, the introduction with a link to the Business AI Diagnostic page (`/business-ai-dashboard`) followed by the three benefits stacked vertically (Builds a comprehensive fact base / Separates root causes from symptoms / Identifies priorities); on the right, the dimensions and failure modes table. Top alignment keeps the layout steady when the table expands. On mobile the order is introduction, benefits, then table. Below both columns, full width: the deliverables list; and the footnote “*The planning cycle is typically 3 months.”, referenced by the asterisk after “next planning cycle”. No image placeholder: the table is this section's visual.
3. **AI Planning** (`#ai-planning`, “Step 02”) — introduction with an image placeholder beside it; four cards (Target setting, Priority use cases and product ownership, Workflow redesign, Quality ownership), each with its description and a “What Signal brings to this service” list; the deliverables list.
4. **AI Delivery** (`#ai-delivery`, “Step 03”) — introduction with an image placeholder beside it; four cards (Product delivery, Knowledge capture, Quality process implementation, Capability building); the outcomes list.
5. **Final CTA** — the homepage's final CTA component, reused unchanged.

### Visual facts that carry meaning
- The dimensions table has two columns, Dimension and Failure mode; weights are deliberately not shown. Each dimension name is preceded by its number in a neutral colour (e.g. “10 Human Accountability”). The area name and area colours are deliberately not shown on this page — the area isn't important here. Numbers are looked up from the real dimension list by name, so a renamed or missing dimension fails the build.
- Collapsed, the table shows only Workforce AI Willingness, Data Quality and Human Accountability. A single “Show all 12 dimensions” button expands it to all 12 rows in the homepage's numbered order (01–12) and then reads “Show fewer”; the button exposes its expanded state.
- Each service ends in a deliverables (AI Delivery: outcomes) panel in the light blue primary tint, with the copy's heading at card-title size and blue check marks, in two columns on desktop. It is the only tinted background on the page and marks “what you get” from each service; it deliberately does not use a diagnostic area colour, since those identify areas. The planning-cycle footnote sits directly below the AI Progress Review panel.
- Section anchors and the footnote link land just below the fixed header.
- Copy is from `Signal Website Copy.md` → Services, with only typo, punctuation and grammar fixes approved by Erik (e.g. “handoffs”, label capitalisation, the opening sentence's “improve it,” and the AI Delivery outcomes phrased as outcomes). Feature descriptions written after “Title:” in the copy are capitalised as standalone sentences.

## Business AI Dashboard

Route: `/business-ai-dashboard`

The page was previously served at `/ai-progress-review`; that path redirects here. Its current content (browser tab title, hero, panels, dimension grid) retains the Business AI Diagnostic wording. The footer and homepage Our methodology section link directly to this route.

### Purpose
- Explain how the Business AI Diagnostic assesses generative AI across Implementation, Operation and Governance, and make its assessment tangible before someone books time with Signal.
- Show the current 12 dimensions with their weights, concise descriptions and example assessment questions.

### Sections (in order)
1. **Hero** — badge, headline, concise introduction to the evidence-based generative AI assessment, and one sample diagnostic CTA. Visual is a 12-wedge radial diagram (`DimensionsRadial`), one wedge per dimension, colour-coded by area. Operation uses the homepage graphic's blue, Implementation its grey, and Governance its purple. Wedge length is illustrative only (not a real score or the dimension's weight) — see the code comment in `DimensionsRadial.tsx`.
2. **Why it applies** — the three panels (Find what is holding AI back / Prioritise with a realistic view of readiness / Give teams clear improvement targets), styled like the homepage's benefit-card pattern with this page's own copy.
3. **The 12 diagnostic dimensions** — intro paragraphs, then a grid of 12 interactive cards, one per dimension, in the same numbered order as the homepage's hero tile grid.

### Visual facts that carry meaning
- Each dimension card keeps the number and area label above a neutral bold name, with a "View details →" action at the bottom. Activating it by click/tap or keyboard opens a tinted dialog with the area, weight, summary and example questions. The dialog closes on the X button, Escape or backdrop click, and returns focus to its card. The twelve names, areas and weights match `Signal Business AI Diagnostic.md`; concise summaries and example questions explain each dimension's scope.
- Cards and radial wedges use the homepage diagnostic graphic's palette: blue Operation, grey Implementation and purple Governance. There is no Strategy area.
- Photography is not used on this page. The radial diagnostic graphic is the hero visual.

## Who we help

Route: `/who-we-help`

### Purpose
- Show which sectors Signal specialises in (fintechs, business software companies, business process outsourcers) and where its approach also fits (insurance brokers & administrators, freight forwarders & trade services providers, accountancies, law firms).
- For each priority sector, connect the AI opportunity to the specific diagnostic dimensions that typically hold progress back and to how Signal's three services help.
- Let a visitor jump straight to the sector they care about.

### Sections (in order)
1. **Hero** — H1 "Who we help" (no badge: it would only repeat the H1) and the two intro paragraphs from `Signal Website Copy.md`. No CTA button, since the copy defines none. The right column is a sector navigator: three square image tiles for the priority sectors, then an "Also" list of the four other sectors as text rows with arrows. Every item jumps (smooth scroll, instant under reduced motion) to its sector section, landing just below the fixed header. The secondary sectors are deliberately text rows, not images or icons — seven image tiles at two sizes looked busy, and sector icons (gavel, ship, calculator) were rejected as cliché. On mobile the three tiles become rows with a small image, the name and an arrow.
2. **Who we specialise in** — one full-width section per priority sector on alternating backgrounds. A square photo sits in the left column (5 of 12) on every section and stays pinned while the long text column scrolls on desktop. The text column holds, separated by thin rules: sector name and "The AI opportunity"; "Where AI can help" as tags; "What holds progress back" as a plain-text list followed by an "Explore AI Progress Review" link to `/services#ai-progress-review`; "How Signal helps" as a plain-text list of the three services.
3. **Where our approach also fits** — heading and intro sentence, then one smaller section per secondary sector (not a grid), separated by thin rules: a small square photo, the name, the AI opportunity and "Where AI can help" tags.
4. **Final CTA** — the homepage's final CTA component, reused unchanged.

### Visual facts that carry meaning
- Each "What holds progress back" item carries a small label above the name — the dimension's number and area (e.g. "10 · GOVERNANCE") in that area's colour, matching the Diagnostic page. Number and area are looked up from the real dimension list by name, so every barrier must be one of the 12 real dimension names; a mismatch fails the build rather than showing a wrong label.
- Plain text, not cards, for barriers and services — Erik preferred it; the two lists share one style so they read consistently.
- Photography: seven square Atmosphere-style images showing the office environment of the teams named in the copy (not the end-customer experience — no card terminals or similar clichés). The three priority images appear both as hero tiles and in their sections; the four secondary images appear only in their sections. Until supplied, each frame shows a neutral decorative placeholder. Images are set per sector in `components/who-we-work-with/sectors-data.ts`.
- All sector text is verbatim from `Signal Website Copy.md` → "Who we help", except that "How Signal helps" descriptions are capitalised as standalone sentences (the copy writes them as lower-case fragments after "Name:").

### States
- No loading, error, or empty states — the page is fully static with no data fetching.

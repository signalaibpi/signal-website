## Homepage

Route: `/`

### Purpose
- Explain what Signal does and why it's different (frontline-centred AI transformation).
- Introduce the Business AI Diagnostic and Signal's other two services (AI Strategy Planning, Embedded AI Delivery).
- Build credibility (founder background) and give visitors a clear next step.

### Sections (in order)
1. **Hero** — headline, subhead, primary CTA ("Book a free AI Snapshot") and secondary CTA ("View a sample diagnostic"), plus an illustrative Business AI Diagnostic sample card.
2. **Our approach** — frontline-centred AI transformation explanation.
3. **Benefits** — three cards: prioritise, find what's holding progress back, scale what's working.
4. **Business AI Diagnostic** — three cards, each tagged with the diagnostic dimension range it covers (e.g. "Diagnostic dimensions 01–04").
5. **Other services** — AI Strategy Planning and Embedded AI Delivery modules.
6. **Who is this for** — audience statement plus sector tags (Software, Professional Services, Distribution, Outsourcing — exactly the four sectors named in the approved copy, no others).
7. **Founder** — Erik de Kroon's background.
8. **How it works** — four numbered engagement steps.
9. **Final CTA** — repeats both hero CTAs.

There is no separate "Problem" section — `Signal Website Design Guidelines.md`'s homepage section sequence calls for one between Hero and Our Approach, but `Signal Website Copy.md` has no copy for it. Erik confirmed (2026-09-18) that Benefits, one section later, covers that narrative role instead; the Design Guidelines doc hasn't been updated to match.

Nav and footer links other than the homepage itself point to `#` — those pages don't exist yet and will be wired up as they're built.

### Visual facts that carry meaning
- The hero's Business AI Diagnostic card shows all 12 real dimensions as a fully neutral grid of numbered tiles (number + name only, no scores, no color coding, no highlighted "priority" tile). This is a deliberate departure from Design Guidelines' "simplified hero graphic can show a subset" allowance — Erik decided showing all 12 is more honest and reinforces the "12 dimensions" claim, with no scores shown so there's nothing to fabricate. Dimension names must always match the real 12 (see `Signal Business Plan.md` → Business AI Diagnostic). A richer radial/polar chart concept (variable-length wedges by score, colored by the diagnostic's 4 areas) was considered for this spot and rejected — parked instead for the future Diagnostic page's own hero, see `Website/Business AI Diagnostic page/Visual ideas.md`.
- All photography on this page is a placeholder (a labeled gray block) — no licensed imagery has been sourced yet.
